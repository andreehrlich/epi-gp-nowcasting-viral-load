Viral load and wastewater in age-stratified epidemic models

Two animated companions to a doctoral paper on Gaussian-process epidemic models, and on whether viral-load and wastewater signals improve short-term forecasts. Both are built for phones; swipe or use the arrow keys.

Draft research material. Results are provisional and the paper is not yet peer reviewed. Please do not cite or redistribute without asking.

Paper flipbook

The paper in 30 short animated scenes: data, model, forecasting design and results, with the key equation of each step and a specialist drawer with derivations and sources.

30 scenes · about 15 s each

Branching epidemics, mass action and viral load

An animated derivation in nine scenes, from a single infection and the Galton–Watson process to the age-structured model with a viral-load channel, using fitted results for five case studies.
